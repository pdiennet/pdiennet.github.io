Personal Recruiter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Personal Recruiter is a personal job-search tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Personal Recruiter uses the Gmail API with read-only access (gmail.readonly) to read job-alert emails from job boards (for example LinkedIn and Glassdoor) in the owner's own Gmail account. It does not send, delete, or modify any email.

How the data is used

Job-alert emails are parsed to extract job listings (title, company, location, and link), which are then tracked and scored for fit inside the app. Job descriptions may be sent to an AI service to produce a fit score. Email content is not used for advertising or any other purpose.

Storage

All data is stored locally on the owner's computer in a SQLite database. The Gmail access token is stored locally and never leaves that computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, except job-listing text sent to an AI scoring service solely to compute fit scores.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Personal Recruiter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Deleting the local database removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via github.com/pdiennet.
